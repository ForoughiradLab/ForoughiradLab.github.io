**Graduate students:** the Foroughirad Lab can recruit Masters and PhD students through the [Marine Biology](https://www.tamug.edu/marb/degrees/Marine_Biology_graduate_program.html) department at [Texas A&M University at Galveston](https://www.tamug.edu/) or PhD students through the [Ecology and Evolutionary Biology](https://eeb.tamu.edu/) interdisciplinary program. Note that students interested the non-thesis Masters do not have to identify a potential advisor beforehand. 

If you are interested in joining the lab, I recommend reaching out to me at <vforough@tamug.edu> 4 to 5 months before the application deadline (Jan 1, 2027) with the following materials: 

1. CV
2. Unofficial academic transcript
3. One-page summary of your research interests and experience
4. Optional – writing sample (publication, honors or masters thesis, etc.)

**Postdocs:** Competitively awarded postdoctoral fellowships are available through the university with applications due February 1, 2027. Interested applicants should reach out as soon as possible to discuss project ideas.  

**Undergraduate students:** I accept new undergraduate students at the beginning of each semester, pending space. I recommend reaching out to me about 2 weeks before the start of classes for information on how to apply.
